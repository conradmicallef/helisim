    600-CLASS · FLYBARLESS · MODE 2RC Heli Field

    You are standing at the flight line. The heli sits on the pad in front of you, tail toward you. Take off, hover, fly a lap around the field and land back on the pad under 1 m/s.

    LEFT STICKUp / down: throttle and collective (stays where you leave it).
    Left / right: yaw (tail rotor).
    RIGHT STICKUp / down: pitch forward and back.
    Left / right: roll. Springs back to centre.
    1. Throttle fully down, then tap ARM. The governor spools the head up over a few seconds.
    2. Raise throttle slowly. It hovers at about 45%, a little less in ground effect.
    3. Keep the tail toward you while learning. Small, early corrections.
    4. Touch down under 1 m/s for a soft landing. More than 3 m/s, or 35° of tilt, means a blade strike.

    Self-level gyro returns the heli to level when you let go of the right stick. Switch to rate mode for real flybarless behaviour. 3D pitch puts zero thrust at mid-stick and allows inverted flight.

    Autopilot: TAKEOFF spools up, climbs to 3 m and holds a hover. CIRCUIT flies continuous laps of a racetrack in front of you at 5 m and 5 m/s until you tap LAND or touch a stick. LAND flies back to the pad: fast and high (up to 8 m/s, 8 to 12 m, clear of the trees) when it is far out, then a gentle approach and a slow descent onto the pad. AUTOROTATE climbs to 25 m, cuts the motor and glides down to the pad to show a practice autorotation. Touch any stick to take control back.

    Stunts: ★ STUNTS (J) opens a list: pirouette, loop, roll, flip and inverted hover (keys 1 to 5 while it is open). The autopilot climbs to a safe height, builds speed along the field for the loop and roll, switches to 3D pitch for the manoeuvre, then recovers to a hover and scores it on height lost, drift, heading and recovery time. During a stunt, crash protection ignores bank angle and only steps in if the heli gets too low, loses headspeed or the manoeuvre runs long. In REAL sensor mode the GPS antenna loses the sky upside down, so watch the estimate drift and snap back. Touch a stick to take over; if you are inverted at that moment, pitch stays on 3D.

    Show: ★ SHOW (B) flies an F3C-style competition schedule in front of five simulated judges: five hovering manoeuvres at the start circle (square, triangle, descending pirouette, nose-in figure eight, landing on the pad) and five aerobatics centred on the flag in the box (loop, roll, stall turn, rolling circle, autorotation), each with a difficulty factor K. Watch the autopilot fly it, or fly it yourself: the autopilot takes you to each entry, the yellow line shows the ideal path, GO in the status line means it's yours, and DONE ends a manoeuvre early. Each judge marks 0 to 10; the highest and lowest are dropped. Not an official FAI schedule.

    Modelled: translational lift once you are moving faster than a walk, settling with power if you sink straight down under power, the tail weathervaning in a crosswind and kicking when motor torque changes, headspeed sag at full collective and on a tired battery, autorotation with THR HOLD, and a flapping rotor disc that lags the swashplate, blows back with airspeed and couples pitch and roll.

    Assists: The throttle is finer around the hover point: half the usual collective per stick movement within about 12% of hover, so small corrections stay small. ALT HOLD (Q) makes the left stick a climb-rate stick in the air: centre holds height, up climbs at up to 3 m/s, down descends at up to 2 m/s (1 m/s near the ground); on the ground it is the normal throttle, so lift off as usual and it takes over above 0.5 m. AUTO-YAW (Y) turns the nose into the airflow above 3 m/s and yaws with you when you roll into a turn; your yaw stick still works on top. TAIL (V) switches the tail gyro between HOLD (heading lock) and RATE (no lock, the tail weathervanes into the wind like a basic gyro). The bar beside the left stick shows climb rate, ±3 m/s, red when sinking fast near the ground, with a blue mark for the ALT HOLD target.

    Crash protection (PROTECT, on by default) takes over if the heli over-banks, sinks fast near the ground, is about to touch down while moving fast or tilted, settles with power or loses headspeed. It levels the heli, climbs to a hover and hands back control. At 15% battery the autopilot lands on the pad. At 8% it lands and will not let go.

    Sensors: IDEAL lets the flight controller see the true state. REAL makes it rely on a noisy gyro, accelerometer, compass, barometer, rangefinder, GPS and ESC telemetry, so hover wanders a little and the blue ghost shows where it thinks the heli is. ESTIMATOR: EKF blends them with a Kalman filter that weighs each sensor by how noisy it is, tracks gyro and barometer drift and throws out GPS jumps that don't fit (the faint ring around the ghost is its position uncertainty). A downward camera finds the black-and-white tag on the pad: once it is TRACKING, LAND lines up on the tag rather than on GPS and touches down within a hand's width of the centre. The small camera view appears in a corner while landing. BASIC is a simpler filter with fixed blending, so a GPS glitch pulls the heli off its spot.

    Screen: the bar across the top holds the flight actions; the gear opens the settings (gyro, pitch curve, assists, sensors, view, stats, reset). Green pills by the sticks show the assists that are on. In REAL mode the pill under the status line shows the estimator health; tap it for the sensor panel.

    Keys: W/S throttle · A/D yaw · arrow keys cyclic · Space arm · H throttle hold · X crash protection · M sensors · E estimator · U autorotate · J stunts (1-5 to pick) · B show · R reset · C view · T takeoff · K circuit · G land · Q alt hold · Y auto-yaw · V tail gyro · Esc closes the settings.
